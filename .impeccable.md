# False Nine

## Design Context

### Users
Groups of friends, mostly 18–35, playing on their own phones around a table in a
pub, flat or car. One person hosts, the rest join with a code. Screens are read at
arm's length, often in low light, between conversations. Attention is shared with
the people in the room, so every screen has to be legible in a glance and every
primary action reachable with a thumb.

### Brand Personality
Engraved, tactile, slightly theatrical. A premium retro football-card and
arcade-cabinet world: metal bevels, enamel plates, foil swirls, scoreboard digits.
Confident and a bit cheeky (the imposter wears a Guy Fawkes mask under a hood).
Never corporate, never a dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Reference artwork: `public/assets/card-back-*.png` and `logo.png`. The UI must
  look like it was art-directed by whoever made those.
- Materials: gold/brass, copper, silver/chrome, gunmetal; red enamel, malachite,
  royal navy; iridescent foil used sparingly; lime (`logo.png`) as the single
  action colour, rare by design.
- Type: Teko for titles, card names, codes and timers (tall engraved capitals,
  scoreboard digits). Saira for instructions, labels and controls (squared,
  echoes the wordmark, legible at 16px).
- Dark theme, derived from the art and the viewing context, not by default.
- Anti-references: SaaS dashboards, shadcn, glassmorphism, neumorphism,
  cyberpunk/neon overload, casino and betting sites, generic rounded grey cards,
  purple gradients, gradient text, pill-everything.

### Design Principles
1. Frame, plate, engrave. Every surface is one of three physical things: a metal
   frame, an enamel plate, or something engraved into one of them.
2. Lime is the whistle. It marks the one thing to do next and nothing else.
3. Drama scales with stakes. Setup and voting stay quiet and readable; the peek,
   the reveal and the result are allowed to be cinematic.
4. Phone first, thumb first. Primary actions in the bottom third, 44px targets,
   no critical information below the fold.
5. Motion is a state change, not decoration. Nothing loops while people talk.
